User Data Deletion

You can ask the operator to delete information associated with your conversations with the Instagram assistant.

Last updated 22 September 2026.

How to request deletion

  1. Email areen73661@gmail.com with the subject Meta Messages data deletion request.
  2. Include your Instagram username, the Instagram account you messaged, and the approximate dates of the conversation. Mention any previous username if it helps identify the conversation.
  3. Say whether you want all associated application data deleted or only particular messages or details. A short description is enough; do not resend the full conversation or sensitive attachments.

Requests are handled manually by a person. This page is an instruction page, not an automated deletion form. Sending “delete my data” to the bot does not automatically erase your records.

What happens next

The operator will review your request and may ask you to confirm control of the Instagram account so that someone else cannot delete your data. Do not send your password, access token, payment details, or identity documents.

After the conversation is identified and the request verified, the operator can manually remove the covered data from active application records. This includes relevant message content and attachment references, conversation memory, saved replies, and associated diagnostic and processing records. The operator will respond with the outcome and explain any information that must remain and why. If you need an update, reply to the same email thread.

Unsend and other limits

Unsending an Instagram message can trigger partial redaction when Instagram delivers the corresponding notification. It does not erase the whole conversation: other messages, identifiers used to prevent duplicate processing, and operational records can remain. Please use the email process for a broader deletion request.

Deletion from this application does not delete your Instagram account or automatically erase messages held by Meta or other people. Copies already processed by service providers, or held in their backups and logs, may follow separate retention rules. The operator will explain any applicable limits when handling your request.

Stopping or blocking the bot prevents further interaction but does not delete previously stored data. If you send new messages after deletion, the application may create new records.

Privacy questions

Read the Privacy Policy for the types of information handled and the service providers involved. Contact areen73661@gmail.com if you need help making a request.
