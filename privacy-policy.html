Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the operator of Meta Messages handles information when you interact with its Instagram messaging assistant.

Last updated 22 September 2026.

Information received and stored

Meta Messages receives messages addressed to the connected Instagram professional account through Instagram’s messaging service. It stores:

  • Identifiers: Instagram sender and recipient identifiers, message identifiers, and identifiers that associate messages with a conversation.
  • Messages: text you send, automated replies, supported notifications of human replies and unsent messages, message types, and timestamps.
  • Attachment references: attachment types and an available media link. The application does not download or analyse the attached image, audio, video, or file itself.
  • Conversation memory: summaries and details drawn from the conversation, such as a name you provide, language, dialect, location you mention, communication preferences, project requirements, budget, decisions, outstanding questions, and the stage of an enquiry.
  • Operational records: processing and delivery status, retries, human-review requests, errors, AI usage counts, and response timing. Diagnostic records also contain incoming text and generated replies or memory updates.

The app does not retrieve the messaging user’s Instagram profile or username. A name, username, or other personal information included in your message can still become part of the stored conversation. Standalone reaction, read-receipt, and comment events are not currently processed into conversation records.

How information is used

Information is used to answer enquiries, preserve context between messages, adapt replies to the conversation, arrange human review, prevent duplicate replies, and diagnose delivery or AI errors. Conversation summaries and preference records provide automated personalization and enquiry-stage tracking; they can be inaccurate.

The application has no advertising, sale-of-data, or model-training feature. It does maintain operational statistics and conversation-stage reports. Human operators can review stored conversations and diagnostic records to handle enquiries and problems.

AI processing and service providers

Groq processes text to generate replies and update conversation memory. Requests include your current message, recent message history, saved summaries and other conversation details, Instagram/conversation identifiers, and the assistant’s instructions. Memory updates also include the generated reply. Direct media links and attachment files are not separately included in these AI requests, but links or personal information typed in messages may be included.

Models with built-in tools may also use services such as web search. When such a model is used, tool requests derived from the conversation may reach additional providers. Groq documents Tavily as a web-search provider. See Groq’s tool information.

Groq’s published terms restrict training on customer inputs and outputs unless the customer authorizes it. Its retention depends on service features and account controls, with possible reliability or abuse-monitoring retention. This policy does not promise zero retention by Groq. See Groq’s data information and service terms.

Meta/Instagram handles message delivery and receives the recipient identifier and reply text. Supabase provides database hosting for stored conversations and operational records. The server host and any connection or tunnel provider process traffic needed to deliver the service. When ngrok is used, that includes incoming webhook traffic. An optional monitoring destination may receive event types, internal event references, and error categories.

Privacy or deletion emails are handled by the operator and the email provider, Gmail. These pages use no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, forms, or browser storage. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process visitors’ connection information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. These providers apply their own terms and privacy practices.

Storage and retention

Conversation history, memory, and diagnostic records are stored in the service’s database. The application does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period to those records; they can remain until manually removed. Limiting the history used in an AI request does not delete older stored messages.

When Instagram delivers a supported unsend notification, the app redacts the matching message content and some related reply, memory, and diagnostic content. This is partial redaction, not deletion of all data about you: identifiers, operational records, and other messages can remain.

Provider-held copies, including any backups or logs, are governed by the relevant provider’s retention controls. Deleting application records does not automatically remove copies held by Instagram or other providers. Use the deletion instructions for a broader request.

Security

The application checks the authenticity of incoming message notifications and restricts public access to its database records. Access still depends on how the service and its providers are configured. No storage or transmission system is completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information in messages.

Your choices

You can stop messaging or block the Instagram account to stop future interaction. Sending “human” requests human review and pauses automated replies, but does not erase stored information or prevent new messages from being received and stored. To request correction or deletion, contact the operator using the details below.

The service does not verify users’ ages. If you believe a child has shared personal information with the assistant, contact the operator to request review and deletion.

Changes to this policy

Changes to the service or its data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
